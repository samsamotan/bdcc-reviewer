## Conventions & ambiguities
- Streaming in Exhibit 2a is paid subscriptions plus on-demand ad-supported streaming; SoundExchange (internet radio) is not in that exhibit.
- Percentage change divides by the absolute value of the starting number, so a loss growing from -80 to -93 is a change of -13, shown as -16% (the case prints -16%).
- Dollars and euros: Spotify's financials are in euros; user prices and royalties are in dollars. The module does not convert between them.
- The "approximately 70% of revenue to rights holders" is an average of undisclosed contracts.

## Errata
- The case says an article quoted an artist who made under 200 dollars from 600,000 streams over 18 months. At 0.006 to 0.008 per stream, the label would receive 3,600 to 4,800 dollars, and an artist cut of 15-20% would be 540 to 960 dollars. The gap shows how contracts, advances and overheads can reduce artists' pay and is a useful discussion point; it is not a calculation error in the module.
- The 2014 revenue breakdown in Exhibit 1a adds to 14.89 billion dollars (6.85 + 6.85 + 0.89 + 0.30) against the printed total of 15 billion, a rounding difference.
