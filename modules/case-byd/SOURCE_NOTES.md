## Conventions & ambiguities
- Exhibit 4 prints three numbers for "Price in China" and for "Advanced driver assist" but four columns of models. The module reads the three numbers as BYD Seal, Tesla Model 3 and VW ID.4 (Renault EV60 blank) and drops the driver assist row because the mapping is unclear. The Germany, battery and powertrain rows have four numbers.
- CATL's capital cost is printed as "less than $60 million" per GWh; 60 is used as its upper bound, so the premiums over CATL are lower bounds.
- Exports are used as overseas sales for the 50% target, but overseas production also counts toward the target in the case. The module notes this in the section pitfalls.
- "Chinese makers are 30% faster in development" is the case's wording; 2.5 versus 4 years is 37.5% shorter. Both numbers are in the case.

## Errata
- Subsidy figures differ across pages: 3.5 billion dollars of direct subsidies for BYD in 2018-2022 (page 3) and 2.6 billion dollars of government assistance in 2008-2022 (page 7). The module does not use either and uses only the 5% share of the 4,700 dollar per-car gap (page 9).
- Page 7 contains an unfinished sentence: "BYD started producing EVs in 2008. EVs in 2008 and by 2025 was the largest EV company in China". Not used.
- "BTD" on page 4 is a typo for BYD.
