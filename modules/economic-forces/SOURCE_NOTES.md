## Conventions & ambiguities
- Basket: the slide prints the totals 1,865 baht and $160.05 and they equal the sums of the five goods (50+35+905+780+95 and 1.25+0.55+70+85+3.25).
- Debt: the printed "percent of 1980 debt" equals the 2022 debt divided by the 1980 debt times 100 (e.g. 2,389 / 5 = 477.8, printed 47,780%). The examples allow 1 for the rounding of the printed percent.
- Income groups: "over 13,845" is high; "4,466 up to 13,845" upper-middle; "1,136 up to 4,465" lower-middle; "under 1,136" low. So 13,845 exactly is upper-middle.
