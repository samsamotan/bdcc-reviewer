## Conventions & ambiguities
- The tax rates are not printed; they follow from the tables: Alpha 30/60 = 40/20... = 50%, Beta 8/40 = 12/60 = 20%. Both tables are consistent with these rates.
- Table 10.2 and 10.3 totals: the slide shows 62 and 68 as the combined after-tax profits (pretax total 100 in both).
- Subsidiary versus affiliate: the slide defines a subsidiary by control of a majority of the board through voting stock. The module treats more than 50% as a subsidiary and 50% or less as an affiliate. Exactly 50% is an edge case the lecture does not settle.
- The global strategy has no culture named on slide 25; the lookup returns "None named".
