## Conventions & ambiguities
- Table 6.4 columns run 2022, 2019, 2014, 2009, 2004, 1999. Ranks are read as printed; a smaller number is better.
- Combined slide ties are kept as printed (Finland and New Zealand both 2 in 2022; United Kingdom and Japan both 18).
- Incoterm price build-up: FAS = FOB price minus loading; CFR = FOB + freight; CIF = FOB + freight + insurance. The slides give the coverage in words; the arithmetic is illustrative (beyond the slides).
